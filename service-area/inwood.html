Driveway Paving & Sealcoating in Inwood, WV

Inwood sits in Berkeley County south of Martinsburg, and the drives here are often longer than a city apron. A+ Paving & Landscaping handles asphalt, tar and chip, and landscaping throughout Inwood. A lane that runs past a side yard to a garage in the back is a different job from a Martinsburg driveway that ends in one car length. The clay is the same valley clay. The distance is what changes the work.

Crown a long lane, and give the edges a ditch

On a long Inwood drive, water that is not crowned to the sides runs the wheel paths and sits where the lane is flattest. Clay underneath stays soft. The next freeze lifts that soft spot. The base needs a stone section and a shape that sends water to the edges, then somewhere that is not the neighbor's yard or the public road. Tar and chip is a common choice on these longer rural lanes because it suits the look and the length. Hot-mix asphalt is the right call when you want a smooth surface you can sealcoat. Learn more about both under driveway paving and in our asphalt versus tar and chip comparison.

Snow piled on the edges of a rural lane melts, refreezes, and crumbs the asphalt from the side. Keeping those edges free of a permanent snow bank matters here more than it does on a short city drive. That is maintenance after the pave, not a substitute for a base.

Newer streets and HOA drives

Parts of Inwood are newer subdivisions. The county road at the entrance and the private lane inside the neighborhood are not the same authority. Inwood is unincorporated, so the county question is Berkeley County, not a city permit desk. The neighborhood question is the HOA: width, material, and whether a shared drive can be closed for a day. Check both before the apron is tied to the road.

Sealcoating a long lane needs one dry day for the whole length. If only the near end is clean and the far end is still damp, the coat fails in pieces. We plan that work for late summer or early fall, with warm air during the cure. Porch beds and side-yard plantings that meet these lanes are landscaping, and they should not dam the edge of the pavement.

Nearby, we also pave the shorter city drives in Martinsburg to the north and the hillier lanes of Gerrardstown to the west. Contact us and tell us how long your lane is and whether it is asphalt or tar and chip today.
