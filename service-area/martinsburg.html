Driveway Paving & Sealcoating in Martinsburg, WV

Martinsburg is the Berkeley County seat and one of the places A+ Paving & Landscaping already names as a main service area. The work here splits in two. Older streets have short driveways that meet a public road within a few car lengths. Commercial frontage along the retail corridors needs parking lots that drain, not just a black surface. Both sit on the clay-rich soils of the Great Valley, over limestone and shale that do not drink water the way a sandy soil would.

City aprons and clay that stays wet

On a Martinsburg lot the failure is often at the street, not at the garage. The apron is short, the sidewalk or curb is fixed, and the clay under an old overlay stays wet after a rain. Freeze-thaw opens that damp seam all winter. Paving the private drive without shaping the apron just moves the crack to the property line. Before an apron is widened or tied into the public street, check with the City of Martinsburg or Berkeley County, and with your HOA if the street is private. That is general guidance. We are not quoting a fee or an ordinance number.

Where the existing shape is still good, an overlay can refresh the surface. Where the base is pumping mud into the cracks, the old pavement has to come out and a stone base has to go down. The driveway paving page describes that order of work. Water should leave the drive toward the street inlet or the lawn, not into the garage or a neighbor's low side.

Lots, plows, and sealcoating

Commercial lots in town take the same freeze, plus the same turning cars every day. A low spot in a stall becomes ice. Striping only helps if the pavement under it is sound. Sealcoating belongs in a dry late-summer or early-fall window, with daytime air above 50°F while the coat cures, after cracks are filled. A city driveway that the plow scrapes all winter loses its edges first. Sealcoating does not replace a broken edge, but it slows the sun and water that widen a hairline crack.

Front beds and walks at Martinsburg houses have to respect that same grade. A landscaping bed piled at the garage door will hold the water the driveway was built to shed. Nearby stated towns with their own pages are Inwood and Gerrardstown. The service-area page also names other Berkeley County communities that do not have a separate page.

Request an estimate with the street and whether you need the drive, the lot, or a sealcoat. We will look at the apron and the base before recommending a surface.
